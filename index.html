FAQ

What are you looking for?

Pick your question for a short description of the product and a direct download. Everything is quarterly and publicly available. The data guide (PDF) describes all of it in plain English.

Inequality over time: top 10%, bottom 50%, deciles or quintiles?

Mean per household and share of the total for consumption, income and wealth, by decile, quintile and bottom 50 / next 40 / top 10. Two views of the same distribution: as the SCF measures it (income and wealth, 1962-Q3 to 2024-Q1, best for the top tail) and as the PSID measures it (income from 1968, wealth from 1984, consumption from 1999). Income also comes as the CPS measures it, in two views: cps1967 (1967-Q4 to 1993-Q3) and cps (from 1993-Q4), separate because the CPS changed how it measures income in 1993. Use dd_tidy_long for one row per quarter and group, dd_series for one row per quarter with decile columns.

What exactly covers 1962 to 2024?

The model estimates the joint distribution in every quarter from 1962-Q3 to 2024-Q1. Published for that full period: the factors, everything in the SCF view (income and wealth), the balance-sheet items (pensions from 1983) and the national-accounts anchors. Files in the PSID view follow the PSID’s own coverage (income from 1968, wealth from 1984, consumption from 1999), and the micro data start in 1984. Our scripts turn the factors into the model’s full distribution for any quarter, including consumption before 1999 (with a time-averaged trend).

Household-level data to compute your own statistics?

Synthetic micro data for the PSID view: each quarter from 1984-Q2 to 2024-Q1, households are grouped into 1,000 cells by their consumption, income and wealth decile. Each cell has a population weight (cop_share) and its mean consumption (from 1999-Q2), income and wealth. Weighted means, shares or your own groups work as with survey data.

Methodology

From Microdata to Synthetic Distributions

We develop a new method for deriving high-frequency synthetic distributions of consumption, income, and wealth. The core of the method is to treat the distributional data as a time series of functions whose underlying factor structure follows a state-space model, estimated using Bayesian techniques. The method incorporates different sources of microdata regardless of their frequency and variable coverage.

1

Microdata

PSID, SCF, CEX, SIPP, and CPS — each with different frequency and variable coverage

2

Functional Representation

Quantile functions for marginals plus copula for joint dependence

3

Dimensionality Reduction

PCA on Legendre polynomial coefficients captures variation in a few factors

4

State-Space Model

Factor dynamics follow a VAR in companion form with aggregate covariates

5

Bayesian Estimation

MCMC with Kalman smoother produces posterior draws of the latent factors

6

Synthetic Distributions

High-frequency quarterly output: full distributions for all 247 quarters

Code

Code

All code is open source (MIT). There are two repositories, depending on what you want to do.

Use the estimates: DD_data

Short scripts that work with the downloads above. Python and Julia versions do the same things and need only standard packages.

  • Factors → coefficients: turn a path of the factors (ours, or your own counterfactual) into the distribution of every quarter.
  • Coefficients → statistics: shares, means and quantiles of consumption, income and wealth.
  • Coefficients → micro data: build the synthetic micro data file.
  • Group means across variables: e.g. consumption by income quintile.
  • Posterior bands: uncertainty for any of these.
GitHubPythonJuliaDownload zip

Rebuild the estimates: DD_replication

The paper’s replication package. The processed survey files and aggregate series it reads are included. Reproducing the baseline estimates takes two steps:

  1. Estimation (up to 24 hours): fit the baseline model. Optional, since the estimated parameters are included.
  2. Results (under 30 minutes): produce the baseline figures and tables.

The full replication also re-estimates the robustness and simulation exercises, which takes several days depending on the number of cores.
